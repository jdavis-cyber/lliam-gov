Per ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Cl.6.1.3(d) and ISO/IEC 42001:2023 Cl.6.1.3, this Statement of Applicability records every canonical Lliam-GOV control, its applicability determination and justification, current implementation status, and the evidence artifact that demonstrates it. All controls are **Applicable** by construction: the canonical set was synthesized only from controls relevant to an autonomous agent processing CUI. Exclusions of specific *sub-capabilities* are least-functionality decisions recorded in §Exclusions below, not control exclusions. Statuses reflect the hardening build state at P4.2 and are reconciled to `docs/governance/control-catalog.yaml` and `docs/governance/poam.yaml` by the CI gate `ci/governance-ci-gates/soa_gate.py`; capability×impact dispositions live in the PDP policy of record `policy/tool-risk-policy.yaml` (LG-RM-03).

**Total controls:** 188 across 21 domains — 135 Implemented, 1 Partially Implemented, 52 Planned (each pending item carries a POA&M reference per LG-RM-07).
