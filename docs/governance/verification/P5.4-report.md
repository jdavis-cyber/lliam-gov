## Design — auto-suspend only on real drift; content-signed attestation
The cATO evaluator suspends **only** on a genuine posture-break — a
control-effectiveness failure (LG-MO-08) or a CRITICAL monitor alert. Warning/
notice/info alerts are steady-state signal and do **not** suspend, so there are
**no false auto-suspensions on steady state** (the FINAL-gate requirement). On a
break it engages the kill-switch (LG-IR-01) and flags re-authorization required;
recovery follows the fail-closed IR path (LG-IR-08). The attestation export is
**content-signed** — the signature is a SHA-256 over the canonical content, not
a secret shipped in the repo; `verify_attestation` recomputes and compares, so
any tamper is detected (no self-verifying committed key).
