## Design — startup mandatory-artifacts gate at the production seam
`enforce_governance_artifacts_at_startup` follows the established gate pattern
(idempotent per process, merged-config aware via `_effective_is_strict`, ledger
emit on block) and is wired in `gateway/run.py` alongside the control-plane,
provider-allowlist, model-integrity, and audit-chain gates —
`GovernanceArtifactError` propagates (fail-closed); all other internal errors
are advisory. **Complete no-op on the low-side posture** (low-side deployments
do not load the overlay; nothing is required of them). Fail-closed triggers:
absent/placeholder policy identity (`PENDING`/`REPLACE`/`${VAR}`/blank all
rejected), past-due `policy_review_date`, missing policy file, missing/expired/
mismatched/non-authorized authorization record, missing registers, and any
baseline-denied skill re-enabled without a valid signed unexpired CAIO waiver.
`godmode`/`obliteratus` are **never waivable** — a signed waiver naming them
still blocks (LG-SC-01 mandatory deny; the register's own `never_waivable`
list is the policy of record).

6. **Live wiring:** `gateway/run.py` contains the gate call and re-raises
